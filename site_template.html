A "signal" fires when a commodity has either a same-day reaction event (a large move relative to its recent volatility) or is inside a confirmed, low-drawdown trend — both thresholds are the same ones used in the historical backtest, whose stats are shown under each linked stock. "Validated" pairs cleared market-beta-adjusted correlation, multi-window stability, and FDR significance; "promising" pairs have a fundamentally coherent story but haven't cleared statistical significance yet. The track record charts below index both prices to 100 at the start of the window and plot each as its own panel (a stock's move is usually far larger than the commodity's own, so forcing them onto one shared scale would flatten the commodity line to near-nothing); the vertical lines connect the two panels at each historical trend-confirmation date, colored by whether the stock's 90-day excess return over the Nifty went on to hit the 15% target (hover a dot, or see the table under each chart, for the exact numbers).
